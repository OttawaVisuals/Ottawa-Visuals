ER and provider-access sources, definitions & gaps

Monthly ER data come from Ontario Health’s public reporting API, traced from its current webpage scripts. Results are reported separately for six organizations/sites, four patient/assessment groups and 13 monthly periods. A missing month breaks the line. Current data are not backfilled from live hospital estimates or older exports.

The annual FAO series uses Figures 20 and 21 of its March 2023 report. Its April–November 2022 endpoint is a partial fiscal year, marked with a dashed segment and open point. No gap to the current 13-month window is interpolated, and different reporting vintages remain separate.

The historical provider comparison pools two calendar years per point for ages 12+, both sexes: 2015/16, 2017/18, 2019/20 and 2021/22. Pandemic collection disruption affects 2019/20. The 2021/22 point combines different survey collection methods and response rates, so its line is broken. The source’s confidence intervals and caution/suppression flags are retained.

Recent Ontario results are adults aged 18+, not the historical 12+ population. The 2023 definition includes medical specialists; the 2024 listed categories remove them; 2025 asks about a primary-care professional/team and excludes private-pay models. These are displayed as separate snapshots, not a connected trend or a continuation of the historical series. Administrative attachment counts, Health Care Connect registrations, and surveys are not interchangeable.

Definitions: funding, staffing and ownership

Ontario government funding is one part of all public funding. The two overlap and are never added together. Public and private sources describe financing, not provider ownership. Private sources include household and insurance spending; they are not synonymous with private clinics. Physicians is a spending category, not an ownership category or a complete clinic budget.

CIHI calculated FTE = earned hours ÷ 1,950 (1,957.5 in leap years), including purchased hours. Ottawa Hospital fast-fact employee and nurse counts are headcounts and can overlap; do not add them. Nurses are a subset, and physicians are reported separately.

Calculations, estimates and time periods

Per-resident spending = source millions × 1,000,000 ÷ population. Adjusted spending = original spending × Ontario’s 2023 total-health implicit price index ÷ that year’s index. Rates for staff and beds = source count ÷ fiscal-year population × 1,000. Growth = (last ÷ first − 1) × 100 for the selected series and period.

2024 and 2025 NHEX amounts and price indices are forecasts in this edition; these are optional and shown with dashed line segments. “Actual” means the source’s historical classification and can still be revised. Spending uses calendar years; hospital finances, staffing and beds use fiscal years. No interpolation, extrapolation or matching of unaligned years.

Ottawa financial scope and remaining gaps

Financial years end March 31. TOH and Montfort use non-consolidated statements to avoid combining hospital accounts with research entities. CHEO includes the Children’s Treatment Centre and other funded programs. Comparators are taken from the paired audited statements listed in each record; they are not a guarantee of a completely restated series.

Statement expenses add separately reported building amortization, parking expenses and explicit retroactive wage expenses where applicable. Original subtotals and additions are retained in the CSV. Montfort’s net prior-years adjustments are not allocated between revenue and expense. The government-funding line is the source’s named line, not every public dollar: OHIP, other funded programs, capital contributions and separately reported funding may sit elsewhere.

Salaries and benefits are expense lines, not total labour costs or employee counts. Bill 124 settlements, pandemic funding, accounting changes and changing program scope can affect year-to-year comparisons. TOH staffing also includes the Heart Institute and therefore cannot be divided into the hospital-only financial statements to produce a valid cost-per-worker figure.

Comparable historical headcount series for CHEO, QCH and Montfort have not been established in this release. Missing is never zero. Other Ottawa hospitals, community providers, clinics and nursing-region data are not included. ER and survey-access coverage is described separately above. No causal claim about funding, staffing, access or privatization follows from these charts alone. Manual snapshot; no automatic refresh.
