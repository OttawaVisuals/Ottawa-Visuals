Sources, method & limitations

Federal MPs

House of Commons quarterly expenditure reports, downloaded as CSV. Five quarters are retained: April–June 2025 through April–June 2026. Each selection is a single quarter; figures are not annualized. Salaries means employees’ salaries. Contracts includes office leases, advertising, services and other office expenses. Category definitions.

Ontario MPPs

Individual members’ expenditures, 2025–26, published June 2026. Whole-dollar cells are extracted from the annual PDF. Support staff, office rent, office operations, communications and long-distance telephone remain separate. Hospitality and some staff/urban/northern travel are bundled within office operations and cannot be isolated here. A blank monetary cell in the annual table is treated as zero.

Toronto accommodation combines its base allowance and reported overage. Overage already appears in the office total; it is counted once. Total spending is the published office grand total plus the travel-and-accommodation total. Small differences between categories and totals reflect the source’s whole-dollar rounding. Original tables, page references and reconciliation are retained in the downloads. Reconciliation · Original PDF.

What the travel chart compares

Federal travel includes transportation, accommodation and meals in the House travel category; it is not just travel to Ottawa. Ontario defaults to travel between residence and Queen’s Park, which also includes committee travel. Other Ontario options show separately reported travel or Toronto accommodation. These measures are not equivalent.

Riding polygons come from Elections Canada’s 2023 representation order and Elections Ontario’s 2025 polling divisions, dissolved into electoral districts. We calculate the geometric centroid in Canada Lambert (EPSG:3347), transform it to WGS84, then calculate the ellipsoidal straight-line distance to Parliament Hill (45.4218, −75.7009) or Queen’s Park (43.6624, −79.3917). These building coordinates are approximate reference points.

Centres are based on land geometry, not where residents live or the member’s home. Large and northern ridings can have centres far from their settlements. Distance is a geographic proxy, not kilometres travelled, a fair-spending benchmark or a measure of efficiency. No cost-per-kilometre score or trend line is inferred.

Coverage & comparisons

Member/riding records are preserved as reported, including former members, vacancies, refunds and closing costs. Counts are report records, not the number of sitting seats. Ontario former-member closing costs and federal vacancies are excluded from the distance chart. Other part-year records may remain. April–June 2025 federal distance comparisons are disabled because the election changed riding boundaries; unmatched names in later quarters retain missing distances.

Federal and Ontario totals use different categories and reporting lengths. Own salaries, pension costs, ministerial offices and separately reported party-leader/House-officer expenses are outside this view. A zero in an MP/MPP report does not establish zero spending in those other roles. Missing Ontario party affiliations are not inferred from old election results. Medians and totals describe the filtered records, including closing costs and adjustments.
