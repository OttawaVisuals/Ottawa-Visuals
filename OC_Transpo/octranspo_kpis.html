About the data

Where these numbers come from

  • Sources: OC Transpo performance reports (2020, mid-2021, 2021), the September 2023 OC Transpo update, the 5-Year Roadmap scorecards (2024, 2025, 2026 editions), and inquiry answers to Transit Committee, all from the City's eScribe meeting records.
  • Gaps are real: after 2021, OC Transpo's monthly charts are published as images, so their values can't be read. Monthly figures for 2022 onward exist only where a presentation printed the numbers.
  • Revisions: when two reports give the same month different values, the later report is used. Every figure is listed with its source report and page in kpi_observations.csv.
Sources: City of Ottawa Transit Commission / Transit Committee meeting records (pub-ottawa.escribemeetings.com), 2019–2026. Extraction method, coverage and caveats: OC_Transpo/README.md. Data: kpi_series.csv.
