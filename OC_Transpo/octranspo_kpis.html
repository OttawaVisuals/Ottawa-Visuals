Routes most often more than 5 minutes late

Show the numbers

How it's measured: every 5 minutes at peak and every 15 off-peak, each running bus's predicted departure from its next timepoint (the major stops OC Transpo measures at) is compared with the timetable. Each bus counts for the minutes its sample stands for; individual buses aren't stored. On time uses OC Transpo's window, 1 minute early to 5 minutes late; within ±5 min is otranspo.ca's window. otranspo.ca scores one settled prediction per stop arrival at every stop, so its figure isn't the same measurement, but it's independent. A trip counts as cancelled once per day if a cancellation notice was posted and no bus later ran it. Bus only: the feed doesn't cover the O-Train. Comparison figures: otranspo.ca, an independent riders' site (MIT licence); transit data © OC Transpo open data.

About the data

Where these numbers come from

  • Sources: OC Transpo performance reports (2020, mid-2021, 2021), the September 2023 OC Transpo update, the 5-Year Roadmap scorecards (2024, 2025, 2026 editions), and inquiry answers to Transit Committee, all from the City's eScribe meeting records.
  • Gaps are real: after 2021, OC Transpo's monthly charts are published as images, so their values can't be read. Monthly figures for 2022 onward exist only where a presentation printed the numbers.
  • Revisions: when two reports give the same month different values, the later report is used. Every figure is listed with its source report and page in kpi_observations.csv.
Sources: City of Ottawa Transit Commission / Transit Committee meeting records (pub-ottawa.escribemeetings.com), 2019–2026. Extraction method, coverage and caveats: OC_Transpo/README.md. Data: kpi_series.csv.
