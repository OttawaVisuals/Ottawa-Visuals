Sources, counting & gaps

What the counts mean
  • Officer: one named OPS respondent, counted once across the collection. Witnesses, adjudicators and cited comparator cases are excluded.
  • Case: one related disciplinary proceeding, even where several officers or allegations are involved. A proceeding can cover multiple incidents. A hearing, penalty and appeal in that proceeding remain one case.
  • Finding: at least one disciplinary misconduct finding or admission accepted in the reviewed decision. This does not mean every allegation was proven, and it is separate from a criminal conviction.
  • Outcome: the latest reviewed substantive result in the linked records. Subsequent appeals or court reviews may exist; “not established” is not “no appeal.”
How this collection was assembled

Reviewed named Ottawa respondents in LECA’s legacy public-complaint hearing directory, OPAAC’s discipline and Police Services Act appeal directories, and OPS’s current disciplinary page. Read the linked decisions or official decision summaries; visually checked scanned findings. Matched related documents using names, incident details and proceeding identifiers. Normalized names across spellings; retained separate cases for separate proceedings.

Dates come from the decision text where available. Some directory dates and links are incorrect; corrections appear in each row. The Belanger and Michael Bond dates use the directory because no decision date was established from their PDFs. Incident periods are shown only where reviewed evidence supports them. The chart uses decision year, never incident year.

What is missing

No complete complaint register, officer workforce denominator, or verified total of internal disciplinary actions was obtained. LECA’s legacy list covers public-complaint hearings; OPAAC publishes newer matters under a different framework. These sources have different coverage and should not be treated as a continuous series.

Years with no reviewed decisions are labelled as a collection gap, not zero misconduct. Records may be unpublished, redacted, removed, or available elsewhere. We cannot calculate the percentage of OPS officers disciplined or compare misconduct rates between years. Pending matters shown here are only those listed on the reviewed OPS schedule.

Manual snapshot; no automatic refresh. Future updates require checking new documents and later appeals before changing outcomes.
