Ontario's costly decisions, by the watchdogs' numbers

Decisions that Ontario's Auditor General or Financial Accountability Office put a price on or criticized, under Liberal and PC governments, plus two big policy choices for scale. Each figure is the official estimate, with what it actually measures.

Read before comparing

These numbers measure different things

  • Money spent (ads, Ontario Place) is not the same as revenue given up (cap-and-trade, licence fees) or value to someone else (the Greenbelt figure is what landowners stood to gain, not public money).
  • "Costed" is not "wasted". A watchdog pricing a decision isn't a verdict that it was wrong; the cards say which ones the watchdog actually criticized. Two are plain policy choices, included for scale.
  • Amounts are the official estimates as published, in dollars of the day, over different time spans. Several are ranges or projections, not final costs.
  • This is a short list of well-documented cases, not a complete audit of either government.
