The assumptions

Share of the smaller party's voters who actually move to the combined candidate. 100% is unrealistic — real consolidation leaks votes to the PCs and to staying home.

The merge only happens in ridings the winner took by less than this margin.

Seats that change hands

Read this as a ceiling, not a forecast. It assumes the combined candidate keeps a fixed share of both parties' votes and that nothing else shifts — but a merger changes who runs, who turns out, and which way swing voters lean. Treat the flips as "seats mathematically within reach," not certainties.
