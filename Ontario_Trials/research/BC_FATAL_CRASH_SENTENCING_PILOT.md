## Search method and coverage

- Searched July, August and September crash reporting across Greater Victoria, including the West Shore, the Saanich Peninsula, and adjacent First Nations communities. Included collisions with a reported death and a date and location that could be matched to a specific crash. Used the crash date when the death was reported later. Included a crash with a driver fatality because it was in scope for the crash search.
- Looked for follow-up by crash date, road or intersection, and public incident reference or name where available. A missing result leaves the court outcome unknown. The Saanich cyclist death was reported separately after the original collision coverage. Saanich Police's quarterly report lists one fatal motor vehicle incident for July–September, which is consistent with the one Saanich crash identified here. It covers incidents reported to that department. [Fatality follow-up](https://ca.headtopics.com/news/cyclist-dies-after-collision-in-saanich-bc-coroners-service-71747636) · [Saanich Police Board quarterly report](https://saanichpolice.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Oct-14-public.pdf)
- The Saanich row is the likely match for the CHEK headline. Its fine and conviction details come from secondary reporting. The registry's participant search requires a CAPTCHA, so this audit has zero court-record-verified outcomes. Its published scope includes sentence and disposition fields for public Provincial Court traffic and criminal files and excludes Supreme Court criminal files.
