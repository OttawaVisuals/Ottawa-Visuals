# Published s.53 judgments: search pilot

Reviewed October 23, 2026. Target: Ottawa Highway Traffic Act s.53 trial outcomes,
using decision dates January 1, 2016–December 31, 2025 (ten complete calendar years).
This is a search experiment, **not a complete collection of cases or judgments**.

## Result

No Ottawa s.53 trial verdict was confirmed in this pilot. This does **not** mean
there were none, or that no such judgments are on CanLII. The search did confirm
that published judgments can disclose individual s.53 outcomes elsewhere in Ontario.

## Search method

Used CanLII's public search interface, Cases filter and explicit decision-date range.
All result pages were loaded for the five retained searches. Search counts describe
matches, not confirmed s.53 cases. Counts overlap.

| Search | Cases matched |
|---|---:|
| Four offence-wording variants AND Ottawa | 25 |
| Subsection variants 53(1)/53 (1)/53(1.1), Highway Traffic Act, Ottawa | 5 |
| French article/subsection wording, Code de la route, Ottawa | 0 |
| 53, suspended/suspension, Ottawa, Highway Traffic Act/Code de la route | 84 |
| Licence/license suspended, drive suspend, driving under suspension; Ottawa | 41 |

Exact query URLs and the deduplicated **133 candidate-document links** are in
[s53-search-2026-10-23.json](s53-search-2026-10-23.json). Twenty-seven candidate texts
were checked directly, plus the supplemental Minichiello judgment. Other candidate
entries are explicitly marked `search_metadata_only`, not verified outcomes. The
supplemental Minichiello comparison was discovered through web search and its full
CanLII judgment was then checked. It was not an Ottawa-search hit.

The initial broad searches were exploratory; the five retained searches form the
documented pilot. Full judgments and unrelated personal details are not copied
into the repository. The catalogue retains citations/links and review status.

## Limits and implication for the traffic chart

- The [OCJ explicitly states CanLII is not exhaustive](https://www.ontariocourts.ca/ocj/decisions/).
  Search variants can also miss wording, OCR errors or judgments without an Ottawa reference.
- A reference to Ottawa can mean a residence, a street elsewhere, a cited authority,
  a tribunal office or a treatment centre. The location must be established from the case.
- A judgment can concern a motion, bail, sentencing, appeal or an earlier conviction;
  it need not decide the s.53 charge. Court language must be read for that charge.
- Decision date, offence date and trial/disposition date can differ. One judgment
  can resolve several charges; several judgments can concern one proceeding.
- The OCJ statistical row contains annual charge totals with no case numbers or
  individual trial dates. None of these documents can be linked individually to it.

**No trial totals were reclassified and no conviction rate was calculated.** The
pilot supports a separate collection of verified published outcomes; it does not
support filling the entire unknown-trial-outcome segment of the Ottawa chart.
