Data notes

Definitions: Ballot turnout = ballots cast ÷ registered voters (available for wards in 2018 and 2022). Valid council vote participation = valid councillor votes ÷ registered voters; it excludes blank and overvoted contests and is not ballot turnout. Candidate shares use valid votes for that race. Citywide mayor bars separate valid candidate votes from everyone with no valid mayor vote recorded, including non-voters and blank/overvoted contests.
Coverage: Mayor results by ward exclude citywide special polls in 2014, 2018 and 2022. They show poll leaders, not complete home-ward winners. Earlier years use original ward numbers on the 2022 locator map. Cross-2022 changes are withheld because boundaries were not harmonized. Ward 24 has no pre-2022 result.
Sources: 2010 City download (not certified) · 2014 official · 2018 official · 2022 official.
Reconciled October 17, 2026. Wards 7 and 15 lack 2010 council results in the City download. The population-based view is withheld pending verification of its population estimates. Full reconciliation and limitations · Ward data CSV.
