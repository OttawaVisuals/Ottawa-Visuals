Data & methodology

Source: the PWHL's own stats feed (HockeyTech), fetched by a daily-scheduled pipeline in PWHL/scripts/daily_update.R and pre-aggregated into the JSON files this page loads by PWHL/scripts/build_dashboard_json.py.

  • Includes regular-season, preseason and playoff records from the 2024 inaugural season through 2025-26.
  • "Player leaders" uses regular-season totals only; playoff stats aren't blended in.
  • Team colours are each club's dominant brand colour, approximated by eye (the league publishes colour names, not codes); the darkest ones are lightened a little on the dark theme so they stay visible.
  • Attendance: a game counts as Takeover Tour / neutral site when its venue is outside the home team's own market (e.g. Ottawa's games in Calgary or Winnipeg). Big-arena games inside the market (Canadian Tire Centre, Bell Centre, Scotiabank Arena) count as home-market games. Capacity is the hockey capacity from PWHL/data/PWHL_Venues.csv, so a sold-out game can read slightly above or below 100%; games at venues with no listed capacity are left out of the % figures.
  • Home-page attendance totals add reported game visits; they do not identify unique attendees. Player ice time adds each player's logged time.
  • The home-page season table breaks the league totals out by recorded season and competition phase. Player counts are players with game-log entries in that season.
  • "Superlatives" uses skater totals for the selected season (regular, preseason, or playoff, matching the season picked). Players traded mid-season appear once, with their split rows summed (marked *); the feed also emits occasional exact-duplicate stat lines, which are dropped.
  • Per-60 rankings divide the stat by ice time (stat × 3600 / seconds on ice). Eligibility minimums scale with the season's length — roughly 8 games and 120 minutes over a full regular season, proportionally lower for a short playoff run — so a three-game call-up with one lucky shift doesn't win everything.
  • Team awards: scoring balance/top-line awards compare the top three scorers' goals plus assists as a share of team scoring. Fair play and penalty-box awards use logged player and goalie penalty minutes per completed team game.
  • Salary awards use the PWHLPA 2025–26 salary guide. It lists 23–25 player salaries per team, while the season roster data has 26–29 players per team. These comparisons cover listed salaries only and are not complete payroll estimates.
  • Individual player superlatives use skater stat lines; the separate team penalty-rate awards include logged goalie penalties where available.
  • No "hit the post/crossbar" award: the play-by-play feed records shots on goal, blocked shots, and goals, but never missed shots, so iron is invisible in this data.
  • The shot map, goals-by-period, and event-mix charts are drawn from the full play-by-play record across all seasons combined, not filtered to the season selector above — a per-season breakdown is a natural next step.
  • Coordinates in the play-by-play feed are in feet from center ice/center width, matching a standard 200×85 ft rink; they aren't normalized for which end a team was attacking, so the map shows raw shot locations rather than "offensive zone" shots.
  • Team pages: team records and player totals follow the selected season. Franchise scoring leaders add each player's regular-season goals and points for the club.
  • Player pages: season lines and game logs come from the league's box-score feed. Shot maps include only shots with valid play-by-play coordinates.
  • The Shin-Pad Shooter counts a shot attempt as blocked when the feed logs a blocked shot with that player as the shooter. Missed shots aren't in the feed, so attempts = shots on goal + blocked. The Rap Sheet counts penalty events by infraction. The Hall of Sustained Quirk uses the totals view, regular seasons only, and needs an outright (untied) win.
