How to read this: Team circles count everyone listed on a team roster, including camp invitees and draftees. Purple lines start at a player's first recorded PWHL roster: Drafted, Signed, or Other roster entry. Team-to-team lines combine recorded trades with changes between consecutive season rosters when the player was signed or played for both clubs. Grey lines are explicit retirement transactions; a retirement record does not prove a permanent exit. The 2026–27 preseason roster is included. Missing or ambiguous records are left out of the arrows.
