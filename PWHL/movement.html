Ottawa roster history

How to read this: A player has one dot on every team where she played a recorded game or was listed as signed. Camp invitees and unsigned draftees are excluded. The season filter uses the move's arrival season; dots always show all-time membership. Each line connects that player's dots on two teams: its dots start small and spaced apart at the former team, then grow and merge into a solid line that widens toward the new team. Across all seasons, ten lines are recorded trades; the others are inferred from ordered season rosters with played or signed evidence at both ends. Those roster changes are not necessarily trades. The 2026–27 preseason roster is included. Missing or ambiguous moves are left out and marked as gaps in player histories where visible.
