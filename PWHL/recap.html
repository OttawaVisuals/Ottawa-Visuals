How to read this

Each frame is a game date. Regular-season points use 3 for a regulation win, 2 for an overtime/shootout win, and 1 for an overtime/shootout loss; ties share a displayed rank because historical official tie-break snapshots are unavailable. Playoff results are grouped by round and series from the league bracket feed. The completed final shows the champion.

Game highlights use player game logs and play-by-play: shutouts, hat tricks, short-handed goals and game misconducts. Suspension notices are read from transaction data when present; a game misconduct alone does not establish a suspension.

Travel lines are modeled between regular-season and playoff venues; actual travel dates, transport and itinerary are unknown. The counter assumes a return home after gaps longer than four days, road distance of 1.25× straight-line distance, bus travel up to 400 miles at 80 km/h, and otherwise air at 800 km/h plus three hours for transfers. Attendance sums reported visits rather than unique people.
