How to read this

Each frame is a date with at least one completed regular-season game. Tables and charts include games through that date. Team points use 3 for a regulation win, 2 for an overtime/shootout win, and 1 for an overtime/shootout loss; reconstructed final points and goal totals are checked against the season standings. Teams tied on points share a displayed rank; historical official tie-break snapshots are unavailable. Scoring leaders sum skater game logs across teams when a player moved, with goals then name used to order point ties.

The top four teams qualify. A team is marked clinched only when at most three others can still match its points, and eliminated only when four others already exceed its maximum possible points. Ties stay unresolved until the final standings. These conservative badges can appear later than official announcements.

Map positions use one Mercator scale so geography is not stretched. Lines run from a team's previous game venue to its next game venue. After a gap of more than four days, the model assumes a return to its most-used home rink. Routes are schematic and assigned to the game date; actual travel dates, transport and itinerary are unknown. The travel counter adds indicative team hours for these trips and assumed returns: road distance is 1.25× straight-line distance, bus if that is at most 400 miles at 80 km/h; otherwise air at 800 km/h plus three hours for transfers and overhead. Game-clock time sums 60-minute games and recorded goalie minutes in OT/SO games. PIM is the sum of player game-log penalty minutes. OT/SO counts games decided beyond regulation. Playoffs are separate and are not included.
