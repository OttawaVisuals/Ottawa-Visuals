How this estimate works

  • Games and venues: all eight teams' 30 completed 2025–26 regular-season games in the PWHL/HockeyTech feed. A home-designated game outside its team's home market still requires travel.
  • Each team starts from its most-used home rink. The route follows the next game venue. For gaps over four days while outside its home market, it assumes a return to that rink, then a new departure. Ottawa's December 4 Toronto trip and December 16–23 trip stay separate under this rule.
  • Bus if the modeled road route is 400 miles (644 km) or less; air if longer. This is a simplified scenario. The PWHL agreement, §12.1 also has a six-hour rule and exceptions, which this model does not apply.
  • Air legs assume a direct flight between the listed major commercial airports nearest the two rinks, plus drives to and from those airports. They do not verify actual flights or team bookings. Flight distance is great-circle distance. Flight time assumes 800 km/h plus two hours of total airport/flight overhead.
  • Road distance and time use OSRM estimates. Airport coordinates: OurAirports. Venue coordinates: the site's PWHL venues file. Times exclude border delays, congestion, weather, stops and game-day logistics. The map draws schematic straight or curved lines, not road geometry.
  • Ottawa captain Brianne Jenner described her daughter riding with the team on a Toronto trip in 2024–25. That supports bus use on a Toronto trip, but does not confirm travel modes for this season or other teams.
