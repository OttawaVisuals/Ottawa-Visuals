Seeking witnesses to a serious collision involving a cyclist on Haig Dr.
By Ottawa Police Service
-
Mar 26, 2026
Breaking News
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: Thursday, March 26, 2026 9:45 a.m.
(Ottawa)—The Ottawa Police Service (OPS) is seeking witnesses to a serious collision involving a cyclist in the 2000 block of Haig Drive.
At approximately 6:17 a.m., officers responded to reports of a cyclist-involved collision.
At this time, it is believed that the cyclist may have fallen off their bicycle before being struck by an oncoming vehicle.
The cyclist was transported to the hospital with life-threatening injuries.
Investigators would like to speak with anyone who may not have spoken to officers on scene or who may have dash camera footage in the area around the time of the incident.
Anyone with information is asked to contact the OPS Collision Investigations Unit at 613-236-1222 extension 2345.
Anonymous tips can be submitted by calling Crime Stoppers toll-free at 1-800-222-8477 or at crimestoppers.ca .
