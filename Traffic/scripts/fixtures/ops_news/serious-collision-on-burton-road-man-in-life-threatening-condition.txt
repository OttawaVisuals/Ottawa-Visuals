FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: Friday, October 16, 2026 11:10 a.m.
Ottawa—The Ottawa Police Service (OPS) is seeking witnesses to a serious collision that occurred on Thursday, October 15, 2026.
At approximately 7:46 p.m., officers responded to a collision involving a vehicle and a transport truck without a trailer in the 700 block of Burton Road.
A 20-year-old man, the sole occupant of the vehicle, was transported to hospital in critical condition. He remains in life-threatening condition.
The driver of the transport did not sustain any injuries.
Police are asking anyone who witnessed the collision and has not yet spoken with investigators, or anyone who may have dashcam or video footage of the incident, to come forward.
Anyone with information is asked to contact the OPS Fatal Collision Investigation Unit at 613-236-1222, extension 2345.
Anonymous tips can be submitted by calling Crime Stoppers toll-free at 1-800-222-8477 or online at crimestoppers.ca .
