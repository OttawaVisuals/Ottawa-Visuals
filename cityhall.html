Read this first. The minutes name who voted which way for only a small share of motions: about 350 of roughly 9,400 since 2022 (about 190 at City Council). Most motions are recorded as just "Carried", with no names, and nearly all of the named votes had at least one member against. So this is how members voted on the contested motions, not their full voting record. "On the losing side" means the member voted with the smaller group; tied votes are not counted. Absences and declared conflicts are not counted as votes.

Source: City of Ottawa eScribe meeting portal (pub-ottawa.escribemeetings.com), indexed weekly.
