Who stood behind each council vote?

Each councillor was elected by a different number of ballots in a ward of a different size. This page adds up the 2022 ballots behind the councillors on each side of every recorded City Council vote, to see how often the side with fewer voters behind it still won.

How to read this. "Ballots" means votes cast for the winning councillor in their ward in October 2022 (turnout was 44%). A 13-to-12 vote can be won by the 13 even when the 12 were elected with more total ballots, because wards differ in size and turnout. This is a weighting exercise, not a claim that votes should count by ballots: councillors are elected to represent whole wards, including non-voters.
