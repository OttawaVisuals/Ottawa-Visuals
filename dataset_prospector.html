Combo plays

Where the value is — combinations nobody has published, in the spirit of the well-records work.

C1

Waste-heat atlas

GHGRP × NPRI × ODB footprints × OEB CCIM feeders

Rank large combustion facilities by recoverable heat near electrically constrained feeders. The missing third leg of the geothermal/solar/waste-heat map.

C2

Retrofit market map

EnerGuide (have) × ODB year-built + floors × Ottawa building permits

Which FSAs hold old, large, un-retrofitted stock — a direct extension of the Retrofit Explorer.

C3

Transit truth machine

GTFS-RT logging (Pi) × eScribe KPI scrape

Log vehicle positions every 30 s for a few months and compute independent on-time performance to compare against OC Transpo's own KPIs.

C4

RTO impact panel

TBS NCR headcounts × 311 volumes × IESO Ottawa-zone load × parking/office data

The quantified backbone for the quality-of-life / mayoral-election piece.

C5

Deep vs. shallow geothermal

Water wells (have) × petroleum formation tops × LiDAR DTM

Overburden thickness and bedrock context for the Ottawa pilot — petroleum wells are the deeper sibling of the records already mined.
