Federal contracts · management consulting · 2017–2025

What Ottawa's federal government pays management consultants

Every federal contract over $10,000 is published. This page counts the ones coded as management and business consulting: how much, how many, and which departments. It describes spending. It does not show whether any of it was needed or duplicated in-house work.

Spend by year

Contract value by the year on the contract. Two bands because federal procurement moved from one coding system (GSIN) to another (UNSPSC) from about 2021. Counting only the old codes made consulting look like it collapsed after 2021. Counting both is what makes the line continuous.

What this can't show. (1) IT consulting: we left it out. The old IT codes are a narrow slice of IT work and the new UNSPSC IT family is much broader, so no series spans the 2021 change honestly. (2) 2026 is left out because the data only reaches June 2026 and recent months are filled in late. (3) Contracts under $10,000 are published only as department totals. (4) Counts are disclosure records, and amendments can appear as their own records, so they're not the same as the number of distinct contracts. (5) Coding is by the department. A contract coded as management consulting may not be what a reader would call consulting, and some consulting is coded elsewhere. (6) Nothing here links spending to return-to-office decisions. The 2026 data doesn't yet reach the July mandate.

Source: Government of Canada, Proactive Disclosure of Contracts (contracts over $10,000), downloaded 2026-10-19; reliable through June 2026. Management consulting = a hand-checked list of GSIN codes plus UNSPSC codes beginning 8010 (management advisory services). Year is the contract date; value is the disclosed contract value.
