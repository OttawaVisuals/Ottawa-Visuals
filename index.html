About this project

Ottawa Visuals is a personal project. I take publicly available data about the city and province — traffic, collisions, elections, housing, climate — and turn it into Power BI reports or data visualizations anyone can poke around in.

I'm a data enthusiast and a visual learner, and this site is where I share the visuals I make. Questions about the data, or ideas for a new one? Reach me at visualsottawa@gmail.com.
