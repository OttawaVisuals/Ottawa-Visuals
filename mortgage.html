Methodology & assumptions

Mortgage payment

Standard Canadian mortgage formula with semi-annual compounding (as required by the Interest Act). Bi-weekly rate: r_bw = (1 + rate/200)^(2/26) − 1. Payment: P × r_bw / (1 − (1+r_bw)^−n) where n = years × 26.

CMHC stress test & bank maximum

GDS ratio calculated at both contract rate and stress rate (max of rate+2% and 5.25%, per OSFI B-20). GDS = (mortgage + property tax + heat) / gross monthly income. Bank maximum uses GDS ≤ 32% at stress rate — the binding constraint for most borrowers.

Net income estimate

Combined federal + provincial income tax, CPP and EI premiums, net of the Canada Workers Benefit. Rates from TaxTips.ca 2021 employment income comparison, province-inferred from postal code. Not a substitute for a tax calculator.

Income distribution

Source: Statistics Canada 2021 Census Profile by forward sortation area (FSA). Shows the share of people with employment income in each bracket. Compares individual employment income against FSA, province, and Canada distributions.

CPI adjustment

Source: Statistics Canada table 18-10-0005-01 (annual average, all-items, Canada, base 2002=100). Used to inflation-adjust the 2005 Ottawa benchmark price to today's dollars for comparison.

Ottawa housing data

Weekly sales data from OREB via the OttawaVisuals open dataset. CREA MLS HPI benchmark prices. Trailing 12-month bracket distribution aggregated from weekly reports.
