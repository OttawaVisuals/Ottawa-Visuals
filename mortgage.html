Methodology & assumptions

Mortgage payment

Standard Canadian mortgage formula with semi-annual compounding (as required by the Interest Act). Bi-weekly rate: r_bw = (1 + rate/200)^(2/26) − 1. Payment: P × r_bw / (1 − (1+r_bw)^−n) where n = years × 26.

CMHC stress test & bank maximum

GDS ratio calculated at both contract rate and stress rate (max of rate+2% and 5.25%, per OSFI B-20). GDS = (mortgage + property tax + heat) / gross monthly income. TDS adds all other monthly debt payments. Bank qualifying limits: GDS ≤ 39% and TDS ≤ 44% at stress rate.

Net income estimate

Combined federal + provincial income tax, CPP and EI premiums. Rates from TaxTips.ca 2026 combined marginal brackets (Other Income column), province-inferred from postal code. Not a substitute for a tax calculator.

Neighbourhood profile

Source: Statistics Canada 2021 Census Profile by forward sortation area (FSA).

CPI adjustment

Source: Statistics Canada table 18-10-0005-01 (annual average, all-items, Canada, base 2002=100). Used to inflation-adjust the 2005 Ottawa benchmark price to today's dollars for comparison.
