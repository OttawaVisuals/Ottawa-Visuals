How many voters stood behind each Ontario vote?

Each MPP was elected by a different number of ballots. This page adds up the ballots that elected the MPPs on each side of every recorded division, and compares them with all registered electors in Ontario.

What this does and doesn't show. Votes at Queen's Park follow party lines, and the governing party also holds more total ballots than the opposition in the ridings it won, so no vote was won by the side with fewer ballots behind it. What does stand out is how thin the base is: a winning side usually rests on ballots from well under a quarter of registered electors. "Ballots" means votes for the winning candidate in the riding at the 2022 or 2025 general election (votes before the Feb 2025 election use 2022). MPPs elected in by-elections have no mandate here yet: their votes are counted but their ballots are left out, and those divisions are marked "partial".
