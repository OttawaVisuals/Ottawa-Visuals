Recent extremes, hour by hour (1953–present)

Wind, humidex and wind-chill only exist at hourly resolution, so this record is shorter than the 1889 daily one — but still 70+ years. Hot/tropical are shown as hours here, not days — the same underlying threshold as the daily chart above, but counting actual duration instead of just whether a day tipped over the line at all.

Hourly precipitation amount is not usable here — it's essentially unrecorded for this station in every decade checked, 1953–present — so no rainfall-intensity index is shown. Precipitation totals above come from the daily record instead.

Max wind speed is the station's own hourly reading, not a storm-chasing measurement — it won't catch a tornado unless the funnel happens to pass directly over the sensor. The Sept 21, 2018 Ottawa–Gatineau EF3 tornado, for example, shows up as an unremarkable 50 km/h that hour: the touchdown path was several km from the station, and an hourly-averaged reading smooths out anything that lasts only seconds to minutes anyway. Station wind records are a poor instrument for tracking tornado trends — that needs damage-survey data, which this dataset doesn't have.

Data & methodology

Source: Environment and Climate Change Canada, climate.weather.gc.ca (public, no API key).

  • Daily record (1889–present): Ottawa CDA station, which is still active today; a modern airport station only fills specific missing days, never a wholesale switch-over.
  • Hourly record (1953–present): the historic Ottawa Macdonald-Cartier Intl A station (1953–2011) handing off to the modern station (2012–present).
  • Thresholds used: hot day/hour ≥ 30 °C, extreme cold ≤ −25 °C, tropical night/hour ≥ 20 °C (tropical hours counted 21:00–05:59), heavy-rain day ≥ 25 mm, high-wind hour ≥ 50 km/h, extreme humidex ≥ 40, extreme wind chill ≤ −35.
  • Thunderstorm severity: ECCC's hourly weather-condition text distinguishes plain "Thunderstorms" from storms tagged "Hail" or "Heavy Thunderstorms" — a day only counts as severe if one of those stronger tags appears. A bare thunderstorm flag alone says nothing about intensity, so both are shown separately rather than lumped together.
  • A year is only plotted in full-record trend lines once it has enough valid observations to be considered complete (partial start/current years are excluded from trend statistics, though shown where noted).
